About Us

Pak Knives Javed Brother is a manufacturer and exporter of premium handmade Damascus steel products from Nizamabad, Wazirabad, Punjab, Pakistan.

We specialize in handmade knives, hunting knives, folding knives, Bowie knives, kitchen knives, swords, axes and custom cutlery.

Our products are manufactured with attention to craftsmanship, finishing, quality and customer requirements. Custom and OEM manufacturing is also available.
