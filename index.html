About Us

Pak Knives Javed Brother is a premium manufacturer and exporter of handmade Damascus steel knives, swords, axes, and custom blades based in Wazirabad, Pakistan. Our craftsmen combine traditional forging techniques with modern quality standards to produce durable, sharp, and visually stunning products for customers around the world.

We welcome wholesale, OEM, and private label orders and are committed to providing competitive pricing, reliable quality, and professional customer service.
