Manufacturing Process

01. Raw Material Selection

We carefully select premium Damascus Steel, D2 Steel, J2 Steel, Stainless Steel and High Carbon Steel.

02. Forging

Every blade is hand forged by experienced craftsmen using traditional techniques.

03. Grinding & Heat Treatment

Blades are heat treated and ground for excellent hardness, durability and sharpness.

04. Handle & Finishing

Handles are crafted from premium wood, horn, bone, resin and micarta with professional finishing.
